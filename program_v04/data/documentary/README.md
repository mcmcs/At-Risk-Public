# Documentary survey inputs

These two CSV files are the versioned, author-curated source for manuscript Tables 21 and 22. They reproduce the displayed rows; they are not the output of a live or open-ended literature search.

- `literature_survey_ijf.csv`: 24 International Journal of Forecasting rows.
- `literature_survey_nonijf.csv`: 10 additions from other outlets.

The survey cutoff is the manuscript state audited on 2026-08-01. The documented search provenance is: IJF articles containing “recession” and “probit,” supplemented by the first 100 Google Scholar results for `source:"international journal of forecasting" "recession"` without “probit”; the non-IJF table begins from papers cited in the revised draft and adds canonical recession-prediction studies. Reproduction means deterministic rendering of these frozen rows and links, not rerunning that search.
